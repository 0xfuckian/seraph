§ 02 — FIRST-PARTY MODULES

Built-in privacy primitives.

Each module ships as a privacy-aware contract suite with first-party wallet integration. All share the same note pool and key hierarchy.

● Private Stablecoins

Over-collateralized stablecoins with private mint/burn. CollateralVault, xUSD, OracleAdapter, LiquidationEngine.

● AI Payments

Unlinkable payments for AI agents and metered services. ZKCreditAuthorizer proves credit remaining without revealing balance.

● Private Messaging

On-chain verifiable payment requests. InvoiceRegistry with fulfillment proofs and encrypted memos.

● Relayer Network

Gas abstraction — pay fees in XMR or SER while using either EVM. Intent signing, batched settlement.

§ 04 — GOVERNANCE

Self-custodied control.

SER token holders govern the protocol through on-chain voting, treasury proposals, and module upgrades. All votes are cast privately by default.

JOIN FORUM →

● Treasury

40M SER allocated to ecosystem incentives — liquidity, grants, airdrops, usage rewards. Proposals voted on-chain with private ballot support.

● Forum

Discourse-based discussion forum for protocol upgrades, module proposals, and parameter adjustments. Sign-in with SIWE or Farcaster.

● Bug Bounty

Audit-grade bug bounty program covering base-layer consensus, EVM circuits, and first-party modules. Rewards paid in SER + XMR.

● Proposals

On-chain governance via Seraph Governance contracts. Voting power scales with stake duration. Private voting via ZK ballot proofs.
