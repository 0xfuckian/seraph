For years, the crypto industry was sold a false dichotomy:
"Either you choose total surveillance (KYC everything, public ledgers), or you choose illicit finance (sanctioned dark mixers)."

This is a failure of cryptographic imagination.

In traditional finance, your bank account is private from your neighbors, competitors, and thieves—yet you can still hand your tax accountant an audited earnings statement at the end of the year.

Why should blockchain technology be any different?

Enter Seraph's **Asymmetric View Key Architecture**:

---

1. THE INCOMING VIEW KEY ($\mathcal{K}_{\text{in}}$)
• Used exclusively by your local wallet to scan the public blockchain.
• Uses a 4-byte magic identifier (`0x53455250`) and stream cipher to trial-decrypt incoming note memos.
• Recovers your spendable balance and private transaction memos.
• CANNOT spend your tokens. You can safely run light wallet scanning on a cloud VPS without risking your funds.

---

2. THE OUTGOING VIEW KEY ($\mathcal{K}_{\text{out}}$)
• Used to generate cryptographically signed **Compliance Disclosure Packages**.
• If a tax authority, auditor, or institutional custodian asks: "Where did this 10,000 SER withdrawal come from?"
• You generate a mathematical disclosure package:
  - Consumed nullifiers.
  - Exact unshielded amounts.
  - Destination public addresses.
  - Timestamps and on-chain transaction hashes.
• Signed with an HMAC tag:
  tag = SHA256Tag("seraph_tax_disclosure_v1", OutgoingViewKey, Summary)

---

3. SELECTIVE, AUDITABLE, NON-CUSTODIAL

✅ You prove to your accountant exactly what you unshielded and paid in taxes.
